Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We also use your browser's local storage, which works in a similar way.

## What we store on your device
| Name | Purpose | Type |
|---|---|---|
| pd_theme | Remembers light or dark mode | Local storage |
| pd_bookmark_* | Remembers articles you bookmark on this device | Local storage |
| pd_consent | Remembers your advertising choice | Local storage |

These are needed for the site to work as you expect and are not shared with anyone.

## Advertising cookies
If advertising is enabled, our advertising partners (such as MGID and the networks that bid inside it) may set cookies or use similar identifiers to show ads and measure results. If you browse from Europe, the United Kingdom or nearby regions, these only load after you accept. Elsewhere they may load without a banner, and you can block them in your browser settings.

## Your choices
- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to accept or reject advertising cookies.
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Learn more about how ads use cookies at [allaboutcookies.org](https://allaboutcookies.org).

See also our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
