This Privacy Policy explains what information {{ site.name }} ({{ site.url }}) collects, how we use it and the choices you have. {{ site.name }} is published by {{ site.founder.name }} from {{ site.address.oneLine }}.

## Information we collect
- **Information you send us.** If you email {{ site.email }}, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to handle your request.
- **Technical data.** Like any website, our hosting provider receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We do not use this to identify you personally.
- **Preferences stored on your device.** We store your light or dark mode choice, and any article bookmarks you create, in your browser's local storage. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

We do not offer user accounts and we do not ask for your name or phone number to read the site.

## Advertising
We may show advertising from third-party partners such as MGID and, in the future, Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads. If you browse from Europe, the United Kingdom or nearby regions, we ask for your permission before advertising loads, and you can say no and keep reading. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Analytics
We may use analytics tools that count visits in aggregate. If we add one, we will name it on this page.

## Third-party services
Our pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and our images are served from our own image host ({{ site.imageHosts[site.imageDefaultHost] }}), also on GitHub Pages. Fonts are served from our own domain. Links to other websites are not covered by this policy.

## How long we keep data
We keep emails only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Preferences in local storage stay on your device until you clear them.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the UK and EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and we will respond within 15 days.

## Children
