We respect copyright. If you believe content on {{ site.name }} infringes your copyright, email **[{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }})** with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

1. A description of the copyrighted work you say is infringed.
2. The exact link (URL) of the material on our site.
3. Your name, address, email address and phone number.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We review notices promptly and, where a notice is valid, remove or disable access to the material. We also respond to notices under the Copyright Act, 1957 (India).

## Counter-notice
If you believe material was removed by mistake, email us with the same subject plus "Counter-notice", the link, and a statement explaining why you believe it was removed in error.

## Repeat infringers
We may block repeat infringers from contributing content to {{ site.name }}.
