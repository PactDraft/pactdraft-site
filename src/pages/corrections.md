We want our reporting to be right. When it is not, we fix it and tell you.

## Report an error
Email **[{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }})** with the subject "Correction", the link to the article, what is wrong and, if you can, a source for the right information. We acknowledge every report within 24 hours.

## What we do
- We check the claim against reliable sources.
- If it is an error of fact, we correct the article as soon as we can and add a short note saying what changed and when.
- For small errors such as typos we may correct the text without a note.
- If we cannot confirm the claim, we will tell you why.

## Removal requests
We do not remove accurate reporting because it is unwelcome. If you believe an article is defamatory or violates your privacy, write to us and, if needed, to our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/).
