<p>Imagine sitting in the driver seat of an SUV as the steering wheel vanishes completely into the dashboard in just ten seconds.</p>
<p>The Driven reports that BYD unveiled a new flagship SUV called the Tai 9 under its Fang Cheng Bao sub-brand featuring a completely foldable steering wheel.</p>

<h2>Vanishing Wheel Mechanics</h2>
<p>The system is a world-first 0 to 180-degree fold-away steering wheel that stows back into the dashboard.</p>

<h2>Regulatory Compliance and Steer-by-Wire Standards</h2>
<p>New Atlas reports that China dropped its mechanical-link requirement for steering wheels on July 1 under a new national standard, provided every key part has a fallback system.</p>
<p>https://newatlas.com/automotive/byd-fang-cheng-bao-tai-9-folding-steering-wheel/ | New Atlas</p>
<p>According to New Atlas, BYD states that the Tai 9 incorporates five fallback systems to comply with the updated regulation.</p>
<p>https://newatlas.com/automotive/byd-fang-cheng-bao-tai-9-folding-steering-wheel/ | New Atlas</p>
<p>Gasgoo reports that the wheel relies on a steer-by-wire system rather than a physical metal shaft linking the wheel to the road wheels.</p>
<p>https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/market-industry/folding-steering-wheel-enters-mass-production-ti9-ventures-into-300000-yuan-class-market-2123798267514839040 | Gasgoo</p>
